Heritage

Heritage is where Corsets by MS began: structured bodices cut and boned by hand, shaped to hold the figure and then forgotten the moment they are worn. Every piece starts from a classic silhouette and is refined over several fittings.

Steel and cotton coutil give the shape, soft linings give the comfort. The result is a corset made to be seen, not hidden.

Bridal

Bridal in red is a celebration of colour. Instead of white, a fitted red corset dress and a hand-finished veil, made to move with the bride from the ceremony to the very last photograph.

Each dress is made to measure, with the embroidery chosen together so that the day feels like her own.

Studio

Studio sessions are where a design meets the person who will wear it. Soft light, a plain wall and nothing to hide behind: just the line of the garment and how it sits.

We use these sessions to check the fit, adjust the tailoring and capture the pieces exactly as they look in real life.

Atelier

Inside the atelier, every garment begins as a sketch. Pencil lines on craft paper turn into patterns, and the patterns into the first rough toile pinned on the stand.

It is slow, careful work, and it is the reason each piece fits the way it does. Nothing leaves the atelier until it has been tried, adjusted and tried again.

Occasion

Occasion wear is made for the evenings that matter: engagements, galas, birthdays and everything in between. Clean lines, a defined waist and fabric that catches the light.
