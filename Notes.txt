CORSETS BY MS - WEBSITE NOTES
=============================

THE CAROUSEL (homepage)
-----------------------
Purpose: the carousel is the brand's PORTFOLIO. It shows the work.

- It is the first thing people see, so it carries the "big fashion house" feeling:
  images first, very few words, lots of space, no prices.
- It shows the craft and quality of the pieces (heritage, bridal, studio, atelier, occasion)
  before a customer ever meets MS.
- Each picture opens a short article that tells the story behind the piece
  (storytelling over selling).
- It should lead people towards booking an appointment or visiting the Shop.

It must be easy to update:
- New pieces will be added over time, so the portfolio has to stay current.
- Plan: build a back end so pieces (photo, title, headline, article text) can be added
  without editing the code.
- Still to decide: who will log into the back end (you, MS, or both).
